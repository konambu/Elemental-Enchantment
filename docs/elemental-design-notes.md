# Elemental design notes

## Ice

- Keep the current ice effect experiments (`EE_IceFrost` material and texture, plus the `SB_Prototype_IceBlade.pu` edits) for further visual work.
- Daylight can make the look appear muddy or green. Preview lighting does not match Kenshi's in-game lighting; judge color in the game.

## Earth (not implemented)

- Visual reference: an earlier ice-blade test looked more like stone or rock than ice. Keep that rocky appearance in mind when designing Earth effects.
- Gameplay is undecided. Ideas raised so far include blunt damage and a defensive or stun-resistance bonus; neither is final.
